Buyer type
Company standard investment / rental: Most property companies pay standard residential rates plus the 5% additional dwellings surcharge on every band. Professional tax advice is strongly recommended.
Company ATED / non-qualifying use: A flat rate of 17% applies to the entire purchase price above £500,000 for personal or non-qualifying use. ATED will also apply annually. Seek specialist advice.
Non-UK resident surcharge: A 2% surcharge is added on top of all other SDLT rates where you were not present in the UK for at least 183 days in the 12 months before purchase.
Replacing main residence: If your previous home has already been sold, the 5% additional surcharge does not apply.
New leasehold property: This calculation covers the lease premium only. Additional SDLT may be payable on the net present value of rent where the NPV exceeds £125,000. Ask your solicitor to calculate this separately.
